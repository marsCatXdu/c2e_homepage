Yiyi received her B.Eng. degree in Communication Engineering, and Master degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China in 2020 and 2022, respectively. She is currently a PhD student at HKUST(GZ) under the supervision of Professor Danny Tsang and Professor Vincent Kin Nang LAU. Her research interests include deep learning and wireless communication.
