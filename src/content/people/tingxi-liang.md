Tingxi received his master’s degree in Communication Systems from Sorbonne – Pierre and Marie Curie University in 2023. He is currently pursuing his Ph.D. degree at HKUST(GZ) under the supervision of Professor Danny Tsang and Professor Frankie Chi Yuk CHIU. His research focuses on wireless communication and wireless power transfer, evolves through physical layer engineering.
