Yulan Yuan received her master degree in Information and Communication Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, China, in 2022. She is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree at HKUST(GZ) under the supervision of Professor Vincent Kin Nang LAU and Professor Danny Tsang. Her research interests are in the areas of stochastic optimization and federated learning.
