Taoning received his Double Honor’s Bachelor’s Degree from University of Leeds and Southwest Jiaotong University in 2021, majoring in Electrical and Electronic Engineering. He then obtained a Master Degree in Telecommunications from HKUST, CWB in 2022. Now he is pursuing a Ph.D. degree at HKUST(GZ), under the supervision of Professor Danny Tsang.
His current researches focus on Wireless Power Transfer and applications.
