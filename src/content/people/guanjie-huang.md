Guanjie received his BEng degree in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Chengdu, China, in June 2018. He graduated with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from Australian National University in Dec 2020. Before he joined the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou) as a Ph.D. student, he was a full-time Deep Learning Algorithm Engineer.
His current research interests include Few-shot learning, multi-modality learning, and Generative AI.
