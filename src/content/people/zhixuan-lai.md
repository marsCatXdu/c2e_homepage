Zhixuan received her B.Eng. degree and Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China, in 2023 and 2025, respectively. She is currently a PhD student at HKUST(GZ) under the supervision of Professor Danny Tsang and Professor Wei HAN. Her research interests include grid-forming inverter and data-driven control.
