Tailin received his B.Eng. degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation Engineering from Sichuan University, Chengdu, China in June 2018, and his Master degree in Electrical Engineering from South China University of Technology, Guangzhou, China in June 2021. He joined HKUST as a Ph.D. student in August 2021, under the supervision of Professor Danny Tsang and Professor Jun Zhang. His research interests lie in Federated Learning, Decentralized Learning, and Smart Grids.
