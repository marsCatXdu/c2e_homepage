Dr. Tian Liu , a Ph.D. graduate from the C2E Group, will join Huawei Technologies Co. Ltd., Shenzhen, China.

Tian joined the C2E Group as a Ph.D. student in September 2014. He conducted research on models and algorithms for the optimization in power systems with Prof. Danny Tsang. He successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis in September 2018.

Below is a photo of our recent get-together for Tian’s farewell lunch. We wish Tian success in all his future endeavors!
