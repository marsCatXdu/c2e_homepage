Prof. Keith Ross , Dean of Engineering and Computer Science, New York University Shanghai, and the Leonard J. Shustek Chair Professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at New York University, visited our group on Nov 30, 2015.

Prof. Keith Ross is a fellow of ACM and IEEE. He is the co-author of the popular textbook, Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach Featuring the Internet. He was the Ph.D. supervisor of Prof. Danny Tsang .

(People in the photo from left to right: Jason, Bo, Su, Tengyi, Naeimeh, Prof. Keith Ross, Prof. Danny Tsang, Felix, Yuxuan, Sareh, Ao, Tian)
