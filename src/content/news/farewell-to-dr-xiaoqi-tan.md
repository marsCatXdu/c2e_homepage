Dr. Xiaoqi Tan , a Ph.D. graduate from the C2E Group, will join the Edward S. Rogers Sr. Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Toronto, Toronto, ON, Canada, as a Postdoctoral Fellow, in the coming July.

Xiaoqi joined the C2E Group as a Ph.D. student in September 2012. He conducted research on smart grid modeling and optimization with Prof. Danny Tsang. He was a fellow in School of Engineering and Applied Science at Harvard University. He successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis in January 2018 and became a Postdoctoral Fellow in the group afterwards.

Below is a photo of our recent get-together for Xiaoqi’s farewell lunch. We wish Xiaoqi success in all his future endeavors!
