On Sep 5, 2024, at the beginning of a new semester, we had a nice lunch in N+ near the HKUST (Guangzhou) campus to celebrate several things: a warm welcome to new group members, congratulations to two recently graduated group members, and celebration of the upcoming Mid-Autumn Festival together.

We are especially delighted to have Jiadong , our former member and now a professor, with us today. She gave us a tour of an exhibition of her own artworks and Prof. Dirk Kutscher .

(From left to right, 1st row: Ying, Lufei, Xuan, Prof. Danny Tsang, Prof. Jiadong Yu, Yulan, Yuanyuan, Yiyi, and Tingxi; 2nd row: Jin, Zhiyan, Taoning, Ruiming, Zhishen, Guanjie, Jingwei, Lei, and Zongyu; 3rd row: Wenhan, Haoxin, Chengyu, and Ziru.)
