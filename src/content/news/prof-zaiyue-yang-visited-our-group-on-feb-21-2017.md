Prof. Zaiyue Yang , Professor at College of Control Science and Engineering , Zhejiang University , Hangzhou, China, visited our group on February 21, 2017.

Prof. Yang is currently conducting research on the control and optimization in smart grids. He received his Ph.D. degree from the University of Hong Kong in 2008.
